# What is AI?

Artificial intelligence is software that performs tasks we associate with human abilities: recognizing patterns, interpreting language, making predictions, or generating content. It is a broad field, not one product.

## The AI you will meet most often

- **Language models** generate and transform text, code, and sometimes other media.
- **Image, audio, and video models** create or analyze media.
- **Recommendation and prediction systems** rank choices or estimate outcomes from data.
- **Agents** combine a model with tools and a workflow to complete multi-step tasks.

## What AI is good at

AI can rapidly draft, summarize, translate, brainstorm, classify, extract patterns from supplied text, and help write code. It is most useful when you give it a clear goal, enough context, and a way to check the result.

## What AI is not

AI does not automatically know your private context, guarantee truth, or take responsibility for decisions. A confident answer can still be inaccurate, biased, outdated, or inappropriate for your situation.
