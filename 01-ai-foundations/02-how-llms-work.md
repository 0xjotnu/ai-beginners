# How Large Language Models Work

Large language models (LLMs) are trained on large amounts of text and other data to predict what comes next in a sequence. When you chat with one, it repeatedly predicts the next piece of its response based on your instructions and the conversation context.

## Why this matters

An LLM is excellent at producing plausible language. Plausible is not the same as verified. It may invent a citation, misread an ambiguous request, or apply general knowledge where your local facts matter.

## The practical model

Treat an LLM like a fast collaborator who has broad knowledge but incomplete context:

1. Give it the relevant material and a clear task.
2. Ask for a format you can inspect.
3. Check important claims against reliable sources or your own records.
4. Improve the instructions when the result misses the target.
